# Improvement Maintenance

**Status:** Initial operational draft for review.

**Use when:** The problem cannot be solved by a bounded correction or documentation alignment and requires a broader capability, integration, shared interface, or architectural change.

Prefer demonstrated use, repeated demand, successful trials, or identifiable operational friction over speculative completeness.

Before implementation, establish the problem, affected owners and consumers, the smallest viable design, dependencies, permissions, lifecycle cost, and how success will be verified. Extend an existing capability when its purpose and interface remain coherent; separate it when ownership, purpose, or lifecycle genuinely differs.

Keep implementation and documentation with their operational owners. Verify affected consumers and relevant failure behavior. Record unresolved risks and rollout needs.

Improvement does not authorize speculative frameworks or protected changes. Core, authority, entrypoint, and shared-contract changes require the applicable protected-change authorization.
