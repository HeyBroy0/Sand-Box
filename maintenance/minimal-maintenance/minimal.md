# Minimal Maintenance

**Status:** Initial operational draft for review.

**Use when:** One local defect or inconsistency can be corrected without changing wider ownership, structure, or navigation.

Fix the specific obstacle in its nearest sensible location. Follow local conventions, preserve uncertain or historical material, and check the directly affected links or behavior.

Do not use Minimal Maintenance to consolidate across components, redesign shared navigation, or introduce broader abstractions. Route those needs to Alignment or Improvement Maintenance.

The result should be a small, usable correction with no unrelated cleanup.
