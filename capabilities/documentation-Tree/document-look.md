# Document Review

**Status:** Initial operational draft for review.

Before editing or consolidating a document, establish its purpose, audience, operational owner, status, authority, and lifecycle. Check factual claims and important references against their actual sources.

Distinguish requirements from recommendations, drafts, historical copies, generated composites, and navigation documents. Identify genuine overlap without treating different scopes or responsibilities as duplication.

A useful review reports the document's role and owner, concrete defects, source or status conflicts, and the smallest worthwhile changes. Separate evidence from stylistic preference. Do not silently turn a proposal into adopted policy.
