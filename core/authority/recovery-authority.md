# Recovery Authority

**Purpose:** Keep preservation and restoration decisions independent from routine maintenance and cleanup.

## Responsibility

Recovery governs the preservation of known-good states, important source material, repository history, manifests, and restoration options. The recovery area owns its policies, procedures, and records as those are established.

## Boundaries

- Age, inactivity, or absence from a quick search is not sufficient reason to delete material.
- A destructive or difficult-to-reverse change must account for applicable preservation and recovery requirements before execution.
- Maintenance may identify a recovery need or request restoration, but it must not silently erase, invalidate, or redefine recovery records.
- A working task must not claim that a snapshot, backup, manifest, or restoration path exists until it has been verified.
- Recovery authority is not automatically conferred by general repository write access.

## Unresolved authority

The repository must identify the responsible human or governed process for approving recovery-policy changes and exceptional restoration decisions. Until that authority is explicit in an adopted policy or technical control, an AI must not infer it or make an irreversible decision on its own.

See `recovery/policies/`, `recovery/procedures/`, and `recovery/manifests/` for operational material as it is established.
